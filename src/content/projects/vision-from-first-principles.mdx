An intentionally small computer-vision system implemented without a high-level training framework. The project follows an image through convolution, nonlinearity, pooling, and classification.

## What I learned

The useful result was not benchmark performance. It was developing a precise mental model of tensor shapes, receptive fields, and how gradients flow through each operation.
